The End of an Era.

2013 – 2026 · Finland

A farewell

On October 26, 2026, the board of ENCE eSports Oy announced it would file for bankruptcy. It ends the story of one of Finland's best-known esports organizations.

ENCE showed that you can compete at the very top without the biggest budgets. Thank you to every player, staff member and partner, and above all to the fans who stood by the team through the wins and the losses.

Highlights

  • 2013
    ENCE enters esports.
  • 2019
    Final at the Katowice Major. The song “EZ4ENCE” becomes an anthem for the whole scene.
  • In between
    Tier 1 trophies in Counter-Strike and StarCraft II, against far bigger organizations.
  • 2023
    A career high at IEM Dallas.
  • 2026
    The end after 13 years.
